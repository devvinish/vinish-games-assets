# DORRON credits

DORRON (the hero, the Razor Legion, General Vosk, the ten levels, the bosses' names and the story) is an original game made for vinish.dev.
All art, music and sound come from outside free packs, listed below. Nothing here is from vinish.dev's own asset libraries.
The source files live in `assets-src/` (gitignored). `tools/prep.mjs`, `tools/prepbg.mjs` and `tools/sounds.py` cut, recolour and pack them into `assets/`.
