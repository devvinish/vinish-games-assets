# CLASHMATE assets

All game code, the chess rules and the computer opponent are original vinish.dev work (no third-party chess engine).

## Characters and animations
- Pawn "Castle Guard 01", rook "Warrok W Kurniawan", bishop "Heraklios By A. Dizon", queen "Nightshade J Friedrich", king "Uriel A Plotexia" and all 19 motions: Mixamo (Adobe), royalty-free for use in games (not CC). Recoloured into ivory and obsidian armies by the game's shader; weapons, crowns and plinths are built in code.
- Knight horse: "Horse" by Quaternius (CC0), via poly.pizza.
