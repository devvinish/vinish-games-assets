# JAWBRAWL — asset credits

JAWBRAWL is an original game by vinish.dev. Its code, levels, character names, bosses and title art are original. Third-party art and sound were cropped, recoloured, re-rendered, cut and re-encoded for the game. The volcano sky, the floors, the river, the city ruins and the camp tents are generated by our own scripts, and so are the crates, drums, rocks, pipe, machete and harpoon. Every source below allows redistribution and commercial use.

Most sources are **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication). Three are under Creative Commons Attribution licences, and their authors are credited below as those licences require: LakeKeta's Pixel Guns (**[CC-BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)**), Aimee Smith's announcer voice from the General Phrases II pack (**[CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)**), and Tabasco's Gunshot Sounds (credited as **CC-BY 3.0**; see the note under the sound table). The page fonts Bungee and Pixelify Sans are under the **[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org)**. Their licences ship next to the fonts as `OFL-bungee.txt` and `OFL-pixelifysans.txt`. CC0 needs no credit, but we credit the authors anyway, with thanks.
