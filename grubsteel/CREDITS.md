# GRUBSTEEL — credits and licences

GRUBSTEEL is an original top-down bug arena game by vinish.dev. Its code, bugs, weapons, names, levels and title art are original.
The page fonts are Lilita One and Fredoka, both under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org).
