# BRASSHAIL — asset credits

BRASSHAIL is an original game by vinish.dev. Its code, levels, enemy roster, bosses and title art are original. Third-party art and sound were cropped, recoloured, pixel-snapped, cut and re-encoded for the game. Every source below allows redistribution and commercial use.

All sources are **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication), except the Silkscreen font, which is under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**. Its licence is shipped next to the font as `OFL-silkscreen.txt`. CC0 needs no credit, but we credit the authors anyway, with thanks.
