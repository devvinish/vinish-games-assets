# DAARIO — asset credits

DAARIO is an original game by vinish.dev. The code, the level generator, the twelve worlds, the bosses, the hero's name and look (a recoloured suit and visor), the boomerang, crystals, cacti and pines, the water and lava, the weather and the light are made by us. The art and audio files below were cut, recoloured, packed into atlases and re-encoded for the game. Every source allows redistribution and commercial use.

All sources are **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication), except the Silkscreen font, which is under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1** (its licence ships next to the font as `OFL-silkscreen.txt`). CC0 needs no credit, but we credit every author anyway, with thanks.
